# Paw Agenda Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026

Developer: Eren Avar

Privacy contact: [erenavar@gmail.com](mailto:erenavar@gmail.com)

Public policy URL: [https://erenavar.github.io/paw-agenda-privacy/](https://erenavar.github.io/paw-agenda-privacy/)

## Overview

Paw Agenda is a local-first reminder application for pet care and everyday plans. It does not require a
Paw Agenda account and does not include advertising or product-behavior tracking SDKs. It offers optional paid
Plus plans: monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time Plus Lifetime purchase, processed
by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Reminder content is encrypted and stored on the user's device. This
policy explains the information that remains under the user’s control on the device and the operating-system
services the app uses.

## Information Stored Only On The Device

Paw Agenda stores pet profiles, selected pet profile photos, reminder titles, notes, dates, completion
history, notification preferences, security settings, and language preferences locally. On supported
mobile platforms, record content and selected photos are encrypted before being written to the local
database. The encryption key is held in the device's secure storage.

The developer does not receive this content. Local processing is not collection by the developer.

## Optional Plus Plans

Paw Agenda offers three optional paid Plus plans: a monthly auto-renewing subscription, a yearly
auto-renewing subscription, and a one-time Plus Lifetime purchase. Every Plus plan unlocks unlimited active
reminders for each pet and all visual themes.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless the user cancels them in the Apple App Store or Google Play account
settings. Their Plus access remains active for the paid subscription period. Plus Lifetime is a personal,
non-consumable licence that can be restored on a new device with the same Apple Account or Google Play account
used for the purchase. Family Sharing is not enabled for the Apple Lifetime product.

Payment is processed by Apple or Google through their respective app stores. Paw Agenda does not receive or
store payment-card information. To restore or unlock a Plus plan, the app checks the purchase entitlement
provided by the applicable store on the device. Paw Agenda does not send pet profiles, reminders, notes,
photos, or backup contents to either store for this purpose.

## Photos, Biometrics, And Notifications

- On Android, Paw Agenda uses the system photo picker. The app receives only the pet profile photo the user
  selects and does not request broad photo-library access, camera access, microphone access, contacts,
  or location.
- On iOS, the system photo-selection flow and its permission notice are used. Paw Agenda processes only the
  selected image for the pet profile.
- Biometric or device authentication is performed by the operating system. Paw Agenda receives only the
  success or failure result and never receives biometric templates.
- Reminder notifications are scheduled locally through the operating system. The user can use private
  notification text so reminder titles and pet names do not appear on the lock screen.

## Encrypted Backups

Backups are created only when the user requests them. They are encrypted with a password chosen by the
user. Paw Agenda does not store or recover that password. Export and import use the operating system's file
picker or share sheet, so any destination selected by the user is governed by that destination's terms
and privacy practices. Paw Agenda does not automatically upload backups.

## How Plus Payments And Restores Work

Apple or Google processes a Plus payment, maintains the relevant store purchase history, and handles the
payment method. Paw Agenda does not ask for, receive, or store payment-card details. The app asks the
relevant store on the device to confirm that the selected Plus plan belongs to the currently signed-in store
account; it does not create a Paw Agenda user account or a developer-hosted purchase database.

A subscription can be managed or cancelled only in the purchaser's Apple or Google account settings. Plus
Lifetime can be restored only on the purchaser's own devices using the same Apple ID or Google account.
Encrypted backup files contain local records but do not carry a Plus entitlement. Apple and Google each manage
purchases in their own stores, so a purchase does not automatically move between those stores when a user
changes platform.

## App Updates

The current production version receives application-code updates only through the Apple App Store or Google
Play. It does not activate Expo Application Services Update or another remote-code or over-the-air update
service. Opening the app does not contact a Paw Agenda or Expo update server.

## Error Reports

The current production version has no error-reporting endpoint and sends no app-owned error reports.

## Sharing, Sale, And Advertising

Paw Agenda does not sell personal data. It does not use personal data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Retention And Deletion

Local records remain on the device until the user deletes individual items, securely wipes the Paw Agenda
vault, or removes the app. A secure wipe removes the local encrypted vault, settings, notification
schedules, and device-held vault key. Removing the app also removes its app-private local data, subject
to operating-system behavior.

Contact the developer through the privacy contact above for privacy questions concerning Paw Agenda. Because
Paw Agenda has no account system or developer-hosted user database, the developer cannot remotely identify or
erase local reminder content from a user's device.

## Children And Families

Paw Agenda is intended for users aged 9 and older, including children, teenagers, and adults. Children may
use it to help manage pet-care responsibilities, preferably with guidance from a parent or guardian. The app
does not create accounts, display ads, or enable communication between users. Its optional paid Plus plans
are processed only by the platform's store payment flow and are not required to use the core
pet-care reminder features. Children's pet profiles, selected photos, reminders, and notes remain encrypted
on the device and are not sent to the developer.

A parent or guardian may contact the developer using the privacy contact above with questions about a child's
use of Paw Agenda.

## Medical And Veterinary Disclaimer

Paw Agenda is a reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide veterinary diagnosis,
treatment, or emergency guidance. Users should consult a qualified veterinarian for medical decisions.

## Security

Paw Agenda uses device-local encryption, secure key storage, optional app locking,
private notification text, and encrypted backups. No system can guarantee absolute security, but Paw Agenda
limits collection and access by design.

## Changes To This Policy

This policy will be updated before Paw Agenda introduces a new collection purpose, processor, account
system, analytics service, advertising service, or production error-reporting endpoint. Material changes
will be reflected by an updated effective date and made available at the public policy URL.
